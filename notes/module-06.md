# Module 6 - Network Media
Data is transmitted across a network on media.

### Three Media Types
- Metal wires within cable: Data is encoded into electrical impulses.
- Glass or plastic fibers within cables: Data is encoded into pulses of light.
- Wireless transmission: Data is encoded through specific frequencies of electromagnetic waves.

---

Copper, and fiber are commonly used media in network.

---

### Network Media Criteria
- Maximum distance capabilities
- Environment of installation
- Amount of data, and required speed of transmission
- Cost

### Common Network Cables
- Twisted-Pair Cable: For Ethernet connection.
- Coaxial Cable: Used by cable TV companies.
- Fiber-Optic Cable: Very high bandwidth.
