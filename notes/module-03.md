## Wireless Networks
- GPS: Uses satellites to transmit signals. The phone can receive these signals to calculate the phone's location.
- Wi-Fi: Transmitters and receivers located within the smartphone enable the phone to connect to local networks and the internet.
- Bluetooth: Low-power, shorter range wireless technology that is intended to replace wired connectivity for accessories such as speakers, headphones, and microphones. 
- NFC: Wireless communication technology that enables data to be exchanged by devices that are in very close proximity to each other, usually less than a few centimeters. It uses electromagnetic fields to transmit data.

### Precautionary Steps to Protect Wi-Fi Communications
- Never send login information using unecrypted text.
- Use a VPN connection when sending sensitive data.
- Enable security on home networks.
- Use WPA2 or higher encryption for security.
