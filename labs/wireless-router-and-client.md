# Configuring a Wireless Router and Client
In this lab exercise, I learned how to configure a wireless router connection to the three end devices provided in this module:
- Bedroom PC
- Office PC
- Laptop

## My Experience
During the initial setup, I made a mistake by directly connecting the Office PC to the modem. This configuration only allowed a single device to access the network, which prevented the other devices from connecting properly.

After identifying the issue, I disconnected the Office PC from the modem and instead connected the modem to the wireless router using a coaxial cable. This allowed the router to distribute network access to multiple end devices through both wired and wireless connections.

Once the router was configured correctly, I successfully connected all PCs and the laptop to the network, as shown in the image below.

## What I learned
- A modem alone typically supports only a limited number of direct device connections.
- A wireless router allows multiple devices to share network access.
- Proper router configuration is necessary for wireless communication between end devices.
- Connectivity can be verified through successful web access.
